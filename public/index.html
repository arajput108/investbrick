How it works

From zero to first trade

  1. Step 1

    Get virtual cash

    Every account starts with ₹10,00,000 of paper money. Reset it whenever you want a clean slate.

  2. Step 2

    Place orders

    Buy shares, ETFs and crypto with market or limit orders, or put a basket together with one click.

  3. Step 3

    Review your reasoning

    Write why you entered each trade. The journal keeps your note beside the result so you can see which ideas worked.
